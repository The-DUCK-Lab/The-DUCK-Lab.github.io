Ahmed is a first-year PhD student at Imperial College London focusing on neuro-symbolic approaches toward trustworthy AI. He holds a Bachelor's degree in Software Engineering from King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM) and completed advanced AI studies at the University of Oxford. Awarded the prestigious Emerging Professor Program (EPP) Fellowship at KFUPM, Ahmed transitioned from his industry role at Baker Hughes back to KFUPM as a Graduate Assistant to conduct research and deliver undergraduate instruction prior to commencing his doctoral studies at Imperial.
